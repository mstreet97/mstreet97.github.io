While there some penetration testing courses out there are great, some of which changed radically (and for the better) my way knowledge and way of approaching challenges, I'm still a big fan of self studying because of how you can delve deeper into your favorite arguments, plus, reading and learning the experts' tips and tricks is a great way to look at things from a different point of view. I am compiling this list of must read books, and I hope you can find it valuable:

1. Georgia Weidman: *Penetration Testing - A hands on introduction to hacking*.  
Along with the BlackHat EU 2019, this is the book that got me started. Georgia has an excellent way of teching, with visual examples to follow along while reading. The book itself is a bit old and something might not work, but online there are many resources to fix/change what might be broken, e.g. like [this](https://github.com/PollyP/Notes-on-Penetration-Testing-A-Hands-On-Guide-to-Hacking). Either way, if you want to get started reading a book and really get you hands dirty, then look no further!
