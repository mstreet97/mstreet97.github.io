I started out from scratch and since then I kept a list of my favorite hacking and pentesting books:

1. Georgia Weidman: *Penetration Testing - A hands on introduction to hacking*. 
Along with the BlackHat EU 2019, this is the book that got me started. Georgia has an excellent way of teching, with visual examples to follow along while reading. The book itself is a bit old and something might not work, but online there are many resources to fix/change what might be broken, e.g. https://github.com/PollyP/Notes-on-Penetration-Testing-A-Hands-On-Guide-to-Hacking. Either way, if you want to get started reading a book and really get you hands dirty, then look no further!
